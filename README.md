Keystra Energy Group — Enterprise HSE Performance Management
Solution 1 | Portfolio Project | Proof of Concept

Overview:
This project develops an Enterprise HSE Performance Management solution for KEYSTRA Energy Group, a fictitious integrated energy company.

The solution connects:
Workforce Exposure → HSE Events → Consequences → Corrective Actions
to analyse safety performance, identify important HSE signals, and understand their operational and financial impact.

Key Findings:
* Gas Processing Plant — highest injury frequency (TRIF 6.02)
* Offshore Platform A — greatest operational and financial consequence
* Refinery Complex — highest lost-workday burden
* Vehicle/Mobile Equipment & Lifting — recurring safety signals
* Corrective-action closure — closure did not always indicate verified effectiveness
